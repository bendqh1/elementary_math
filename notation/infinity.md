The decimal `0.33` is not equal to one third:

$$
\frac{1}{3} = 0.\overline{3} \neq 0.33
$$

## Notes

* The special notation represents `0.33...` (0.33 to infinity up to 1/3).
* `0.33` is a finite decimal approximation of one third, whereas 0.3‾ represents the exact value of one third.
* The LaTeX:

```latex
$$
\frac{1}{3} = 0.\overline{3} \neq 0.33
$$
```
